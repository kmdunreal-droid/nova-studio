Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy describes what information may be handled when you visit the Nova Studio website, use its browser-based account demo, or contact us. The website does not currently send quote-form submissions to a Nova Studio server.

1. Information you choose to provide

If you submit the quote form, the page prepares a WhatsApp message containing your name, phone number, selected project type, budget selection and project description. The message opens in WhatsApp; it is sent only when you choose to send it there. If you contact us by email or WhatsApp directly, we receive the information you include in that communication.

Creating an account in the website's demo sign-in also opens WhatsApp with the name and email address or mobile number you entered. Login details are not sent to a Nova Studio server by this page.

2. Browser storage and the sign-in demo

The language preference and demo account/session details are saved in your browser's local storage so they remain available on that browser. The demo currently stores the password as readable text in local storage. It is not a secure authentication system: do not use a real or reused password, and do not enter sensitive information. Anyone with access to your browser or device may be able to view this data. You can remove it by clearing this site's local storage in your browser settings.

The quote form itself does not save submissions in the website's browser storage or send them to a Nova Studio server.

3. Information handled by third parties

The page loads fonts and other resources from third-party services, including Google Fonts, jsDelivr and Unsplash, and may open WhatsApp. When your browser connects to these services, they may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser/device details and the page requesting the resource. WhatsApp receives messages you choose to send and handles them under its own policies. These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

We do not currently use our own analytics, advertising trackers or a server-side quote-submission form on this website. Third-party providers may use their own technologies according to their terms and privacy notices.

4. How we use inquiries

We use messages you send to respond to your inquiry, discuss a potential project and provide requested services. We do not sell inquiry details. If you become a client, information needed to deliver and support your project may be handled under the applicable project agreement and legal requirements.

5. Retention and security

Information stored in local storage remains on your device until you clear it or remove the site data. Messages sent through WhatsApp or email are handled by those providers and may remain in your account and our correspondence for as long as needed to respond, manage a business relationship or meet legal obligations. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please avoid sending sensitive information through the website's inquiry features.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit an inquiry, clear local storage using your browser settings, and manage third-party services through their own settings. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you have sent us. To make a request, email khalidmahmood6016@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy

This website and its services are intended for businesses and people able to make project inquiries. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The updated version will be posted here with a revised “Last updated” date.
